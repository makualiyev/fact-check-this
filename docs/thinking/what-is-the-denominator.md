# What's the denominator?

**The pattern:** a percentage sounds precise, but the same fact can produce
very different numbers depending on what you count.

**The habit:** for any percentage or ratio, ask:

1. **Percent of what?** Books, words, people, dollars, years?
2. **Would another reasonable measure give a very different number?**
3. **Who chose this measure, and does it help their point?**

**Example:** the Old Testament is 59% of a Protestant Bible by books, but
78% by chapters. See [the check](../claims/christian-bible-mostly-jewish-bible.md).
