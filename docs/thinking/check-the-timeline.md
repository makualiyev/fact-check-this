# Check the timeline

**The pattern:** a story moves something true from one era into another.
It projects a later outcome back onto an earlier person, or puts people in
places they couldn't have been. Told without dates, it sounds smooth.

**The habit:** make a quick table:

1. **When did each event happen?** Get the year, even roughly.
2. **Where were the key people at that moment?** Alive? Free? In prison?
3. **Does the cause come before the effect?** Could X have caused Y if Y came first, or X was already dead?

It takes five minutes and needs only an encyclopedia. It's one of the
cheapest and strongest checks there is.

**Example:** "Rome backed Paul's Christianity, which became official around
300." But Rome executed Paul in the 60s, and 303 was the start of the
Great Persecution. See [the check](../claims/rome-backed-pauls-christianity.md).
