# Kernel of truth

**The pattern:** few successful misleading claims are invented from
nothing. They take something true and stretch it: "important" becomes "the
only," "influenced" becomes "invented," "some" becomes "all."

**The habit:** split every claim in two:

1. **The kernel:** what is actually documented?
2. **The stretch:** what does the claim add on top, and is there evidence for *that part*?

A claim can have a solid kernel and still deserve "Misleading," because the
stretch is what you walk away believing.

**Watch for these words:** *invented, really, actually, secretly, only,
all, never, behind it all.*

**Example:** Paul was enormously influential (kernel), so "Paul invented
Christianity" (stretch). See [the check](../claims/paul-invented-christianity.md).
