## The prediction

In *The Iran Trap* (May 2024), Jiang predicted that Donald Trump would win
the November 2024 election. *Paraphrased from media coverage, not checked
against the video.*

## How we'll judge it

Trump wins the election held on 5 November 2024.

## Resolution

**Came true.** Trump defeated Kamala Harris, and the race was called on 6 November 2024.

**How impressive was it?** Not very, on its own. By mid-2024 prediction
markets and many commentators already had Trump as the favourite, and tens
of millions of voters expected him to win. Getting a likely event right is
weak evidence of a special method. See [Base rates](../thinking/base-rates.md).
