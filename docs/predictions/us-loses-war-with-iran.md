## The prediction

In *The Iran Trap* (May 2024), and again in interviews in March 2026, Jiang
predicted the US would lose a war with Iran. He argues it would win the
early battles but lose a war of attrition, since "all Iran has to do is
survive," while pressure on Gulf energy and the Strait of Hormuz wears the
US down. *Paraphrased from media coverage.*

## How we'll judge it

"Lose" has no agreed meaning, and a prediction that can't fail can't be
right in any useful sense. So we fix the criteria now, before the outcome
is known.

It counts as **came true** if by 20 January 2029 the war ends (or the US
stops fighting) with **at least two** of the following:

1. The Islamic Republic is still in power in Tehran.
2. The US withdraws or ends its blockade without Iran publicly accepting limits on its nuclear programme.
3. Iran keeps or gains the ability to disrupt shipping in the Strait of Hormuz.
4. A settlement on terms broadly matching Iran's stated demands.

It counts as **did not happen** if the regime falls, or if Iran accepts
verifiable nuclear limits as part of ending the war. Anything else is
**partly came true** or **can't be judged**, with reasons written here.

## Resolution

*Open.* As of October 2026 the fighting has paused and restarted at least
once, and diplomacy is stalled. Revisit when the war ends or on the resolve
date, whichever comes first.
