## The prediction

In *The Iran Trap* (May 2024), Jiang predicted that under a second Trump
administration the US would be drawn into a war with Iran. He compared it
to Athens' disastrous expedition to Sicily. *Paraphrased from media
coverage, not checked against the video. Some coverage frames it as an
invasion.*

## How we'll judge it

- **"War":** sustained US combat operations against Iran, more than a single round of strikes, before the end of Trump's term (20 January 2029).
- **"Invasion":** the narrower reading. US ground forces fighting inside Iran. Tracked separately in the notes below.

## Resolution

**Came true (the "war" reading).**

- **June 2025:** the US struck three Iranian nuclear sites during the Israel–Iran "Twelve-Day War." That was a limited strike followed by a ceasefire, so on its own it would not count.
- **28 February 2026:** the US and Israel launched what President Trump called "major combat operations" against Iran, which grew into a prolonged war, so this resolves the prediction.

**The "invasion" reading has not come true** as of October 2026: no US
ground invasion has been reported. A specific claim that an invasion comes
after the November 2026 midterms deserves its own prediction page if it
gets made on the record.

**How impressive was it?** More than the Trump call. In 2024 a full US–Iran
war was far from the expected outcome. But the prediction had a long,
vague window and no probability attached, and US–Iran tension had been a
live risk for decades.
