# Predictive History: *Secret History*

A lecture series by [Jiang Xueqin](../people/jiang-xueqin.md), presented as
classes for his Beijing high-school students. It has 27 numbered episodes
plus a finale, released roughly between late August and mid-December 2025.

**What it covers:** how power works, why societies collapse, the Bronze Age
collapse, the origins of Judaism and Christianity, the Church as an
institution of power, and finally secret societies. The finale, "#END: Pax
Judaica", argues that a new order ruled from Jerusalem will replace American
dominance. Critics describe that finale as built on antisemitic conspiracy
tropes, so keep it in mind when judging the series as a whole.
