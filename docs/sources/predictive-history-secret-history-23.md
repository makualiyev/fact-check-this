# Predictive History: *Secret History #23, The Organization of Evil*

A lecture by [Jiang Xueqin](../people/jiang-xueqin.md) from the
[*Secret History*](predictive-history-secret-history.md) series. A
third-party listing dates it to **2 December 2025**.

**His argument in brief:**

- Jesus taught that each person has a "divine spark" and can free themselves.
- To neutralise this threat to a slave economy, Rome needed a church that would "imprison the spark."
- Paul, whom Jiang calls a likely Roman agent, built that church. He did it by fusing Jewish messianism, the Greek Dionysus mystery cult and the Roman patron–client household.
