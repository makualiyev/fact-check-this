# YouTube Short: "Bizarre detail reveals ancient connection between religions"

A 34-second vertical clip, cut from
[JRE #2091 with Diana Walsh Pasulka](jre-2091-diana-walsh-pasulka.md)
(about 1:08:25–1:09:50 of the episode). It keeps only Joe Rogan's lines
about the pope's staff, the pine cone and the pineal gland, with
word-by-word captions. Every few seconds the picture cuts to an image that
seems to illustrate what he's saying.

The captions carry a small watermark that reads "@ONE TRUE TIMELINE",
probably the channel that made the edit. One image in the montage has its
own "FACEBOOK | ANCIENT ARMENOIDS" watermark, so at least some of the
pictures were taken from social-media posts rather than from museums.

The owner saw this clip first and provided a downloaded copy. Stills from
it, with what each image actually shows, are on
[the claim page](../claims/pine-cone-symbolizes-pineal-gland.md#what-the-images-in-the-short-actually-show).
