# The Joe Rogan Experience #1255: *Alex Jones Returns!*

Released on **27–28 February 2019** (listings differ by a day). It runs about
4 hours 40 minutes, with Eddie Bravo joining partway through. It was one of
the most-listened podcast episodes of 2019. It was left out of the catalogue
when the show moved to Spotify in 2020.

Also on: [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt9855752/) ·
[Rumble re-upload](https://rumble.com/v1n92t0-joe-rogan-experience-1255-alex-jones-returns.html) ·
[Forbes write-up](https://www.forbes.com/sites/danidiplacido/2019/02/28/alex-jones-and-joe-rogan-made-peace-with-a-bizarre-podcast/)

## What the episode covers

Rogan opens by asking Jones about **Sandy Hook**. Jones says he now
believes the shooting happened, and that he went through a "mild
psychosis" of seeing everything as staged. From there, Jones covers a lot
of ground: Big Tech and China, human–animal hybrids, 5G, "breakaway
civilizations," the Virginia abortion controversy, Operation Paperclip,
George Soros, medical experiments, Nazis and the occult, and contact with
"interdimensional" beings through DMT. Eddie Bravo arrives at about 1:45:00
and argues for a flat Earth. Rogan pushes back on some claims and has his
producer, Jamie Vernon, look things up live.
