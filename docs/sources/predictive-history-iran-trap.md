# Predictive History: *Geo-Strategy #8, The Iran Trap*

A lecture of about 66 minutes by [Jiang Xueqin](../people/jiang-xueqin.md),
dated **29 May 2024**. His central comparison is to Athens' disastrous
expedition to Sicily (415 BCE): a great power wins the first battles but is
drawn into a long war of attrition it cannot win. It went viral in June 2025
and again in February 2026, as events seemed to follow it.
