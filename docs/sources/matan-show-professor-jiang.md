# The Matan Show: *Matan Confronts Professor Jiang For Being a Chinese Spy*

A roughly 51-minute episode of [Matan Even](../people/matan-even.md)'s
comedy interview show, with [Jiang Xueqin](../people/jiang-xueqin.md) and a
co-host, Mike. It was recorded in Toronto. The exact upload date isn't
confirmed here. [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DamEKwS-iBg).

**Context matters for this one.** It's a comedy show, and much of it is
plainly a bit: scrambled eggs pulled from a pocket, a man in a wheelchair
introduced as Mitch McConnell, Jiang calling Xi Jinping his "brother,"
a "sugar cookie" lecture parodying Jiang's style. Jiang mostly plays along
deadpan. In Chapter 8, "Controlled celebrities and spies," the jokes and
the claims blur: Jiang names people he says are "controlled" and, for some
of them, gives reasons. Those reasoned claims are the ones checked here,
taken as stated. Whether he fully meant them can't be known from the
transcript.
