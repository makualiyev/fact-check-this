# The Joe Rogan Experience #2091: Diana Walsh Pasulka

Released on **24 January 2024**, about 2 hours 17 minutes. The guest is
[Diana Walsh Pasulka](../people/diana-walsh-pasulka.md), a professor of
religious studies, on the back of her book *Encounters: Experiences with
Nonhuman Intelligences* (2023). Topics include UFO "abduction" experiences
and sleep, DMT and the brain, her research in the Vatican archives and the
Vatican Observatory, medieval reports of aerial phenomena, levitating
saints, and Silicon Valley figures interested in religion.
