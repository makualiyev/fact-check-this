# МИНАЕВ LIVE: *World Government, the Myth-Free Version*

Original title: **«Мировое правительство: Версия без мифов / Уроки истории / МИНАЕВ»**,
from the [МИНАЕВ LIVE](https://www.youtube.com/@MINAEVLIVE) channel of
[Sergey Minaev](../people/sergey-minaev.md).
[Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/zDuXpuY0SDM). In Russian, about an hour.

**Date:** not recorded. The episode refers to the US Justice Department's
publication of the Epstein files (the big release was on 30 January 2026)
and to Donald Trump as "this year's" star of Davos, so it's from 2026.

**What it covers:** it opens with Jeffrey Epstein as a "man in between" who
connected royals, financiers and politicians, then moves through the World
Economic Forum in Davos and figures such as Trump, Gates, Soros and the
Rothschilds. It argues that the old world order is over and that a network
of people with "resources" (money, technology, data, control of
information) now runs things. Minaev says he'll rely "only on facts, no
conspiracy theories," and that a separate, longer Epstein episode is coming.
