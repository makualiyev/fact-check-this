# German scientists after World War II

When Germany collapsed in 1945, its rocket, aviation, chemical and medical
expertise became a prize. Every Allied power competed for it:

- **United States:** Operation Overcast, then **Paperclip**. About 1,600 people, some with Nazi pasts hidden by rewritten files. Its biggest legacy is the US space programme.
- **Soviet Union:** **Operation Osoaviakhim** (October 1946) deported more than 2,000 specialists with their families in one night. Many worked on Soviet rockets before being sent home in the 1950s.
- **United Kingdom:** its own recruitment schemes, coordinated in part with the US.

The recurring question is **what justice is owed when expertise is
useful.** The US Justice Department's Office of Special Investigations,
created in 1979, later pursued some of these people. Arthur Rudolph left
the US in 1984.
