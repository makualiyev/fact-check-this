## Steelman

The headline fact is true and was hidden for decades: the US brought Nazi
scientists into the country, and officials rewrote their files to get
around a ban on committed Nazis (see the
[Paperclip check](operation-paperclip-nazis-at-nasa.md)). Paperclip
also wasn't the only route. Thousands of Nazi collaborators entered the US
after the war under refugee programmes, and investigators later estimated
very large numbers. Jones's "several waves" may mean all of these together.
Official counts of a programme designed to be hidden might be too low.

## Short answer

**False.** The number is wrong by a factor of about 20, and the source he
names doesn't back it.

- **Paperclip's size:** about **1,600** German and Austrian scientists, engineers and technicians, brought in between 1945 and the late 1950s. Some counts are a little lower, and families came too. No count reaches tens of thousands.
- **The Church Committee** (1975–76) was a Senate investigation of the CIA, FBI, NSA and IRS: assassination plots, domestic spying, the MKUltra drug experiments. No search turned up any Paperclip figure in its work, let alone 34,000.
- **"To run NASA and the universities":** a few dozen of the Germans became senior figures at NASA, above all Wernher von Braun's team. They didn't run NASA, and none of them ran universities.

## The full picture

### How many came

| Programme | Who | Roughly how many |
|---|---|---|
| **Operation Paperclip** (1945–late 1950s) | German and Austrian scientists, engineers, technicians | ~1,600 (some counts ~1,500), plus families |
| Von Braun's rocket team (part of Paperclip) | Rocket engineers who went to Fort Bliss, then Huntsville, then NASA | ~120 |
| **Displaced Persons Act** (1948–52) | War refugees, overwhelmingly victims. Some were Nazi collaborators, mostly from Eastern Europe | ~400,000 refugees in total. Allan Ryan, who led the Justice Department's Nazi-hunting office, estimated that ~10,000 war criminals may have come in this way |

The biggest real number nearby is the **10,000** estimate. But it's an
estimate of war criminals and collaborators who came as refugees, mostly
non-German and not scientists, and it isn't from the Church Committee.
Nothing supports 34,000.

### Where the Paperclip story actually came out

- The programme was secret at the time, but not for ever. Journalist **Linda Hunt** documented the rewritten security files in 1985, using records obtained under the Freedom of Information Act. She later wrote the book *Secret Agenda* (1991).
- The Justice Department's **Office of Special Investigations** (from 1979) investigated some Paperclip scientists. The best known is Arthur Rudolph, who left the US in 1984.
- The 1998 Nazi War Crimes Disclosure Act led to millions of pages being declassified.

That's the trail to follow for Paperclip numbers. It runs through
journalists, the Justice Department and the National Archives, not the
Church Committee.

### "Ran NASA"

Von Braun led NASA's **Marshall Space Flight Center** in Alabama and was
chief architect of the **Saturn V**, the rocket that took Apollo to the
Moon. Kurt Debus led the Kennedy launch centre. NASA's administrators,
astronauts, mission control in Houston and most of its tens of thousands of
staff weren't Paperclip recruits. Jones also says (2:18:46) that von Braun
"directed all six Moon missions." He directed the rocket, not the
missions.

## Thinking lesson

**A true story can carry a false number.** Paperclip is real, shocking and
once hidden, so a big number attached to it feels plausible. That's the
[kernel of truth](../thinking/kernel-of-truth.md) doing the work: the
program existed (kernel), so 34,000 Nazis "ran NASA" (stretch). Notice
also how the source was named only after Rogan read out a lower figure,
and it was a committee that investigated something else.
Check the number separately from the story.
