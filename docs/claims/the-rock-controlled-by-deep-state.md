## Steelman

The Rock really did post about bin Laden's death before the President
announced it. His tweet came more than an hour before Obama's speech, and
he has never said who told him. He's close to the military world. He's
been talked about as a presidential candidate for years, and has been
careful about politics: he endorsed Biden in 2020, then said in 2024 he
wouldn't endorse again. Put together, you could suspect that he's someone
the national-security world trusts, and whose career it helps.

## Short answer

**The story is real, but the timeline and the conclusion are wrong.**

- **When:** Johnson tweeted at **10:24 pm ET on 1 May 2011**, about 70 minutes before Obama spoke at 11:35 pm. That's not 24 hours.
- **After the raid, not before:** the raid was already over by the time anyone was told. He knew *news*, not secret plans.
- **He wasn't alone:** the White House had told the press a presidential statement was coming, and Washington was buzzing. **One minute** after The Rock's tweet, a former Rumsfeld aide, Keith Urbahn, tweeted the same news, and TV networks soon confirmed it. Johnson says "a friend of a friend" called him.
- **"Controlled":** no evidence was offered, and the claim names no act, person or agency. Being told some news an hour early doesn't show anyone controls you.

## The full picture

### What happened on the night of 1 May 2011

All times US Eastern.

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| Afternoon | US Navy SEALs raid the compound in Abbottabad, Pakistan (around 1 am local time on 2 May). Bin Laden is killed. |
| Around 9:45–10 pm | The White House announces that the President will address the nation that night, an unusual Sunday-night announcement that sets off speculation. |
| **10:24 pm** | **Dwayne Johnson tweets:** "Just got word that will shock the world - Land of the free...home of the brave DAMN PROUD TO BE AN AMERICAN!" It doesn't name bin Laden. |
| 10:25 pm | Keith Urbahn, chief of staff to former Defense Secretary Donald Rumsfeld, tweets that he's been told by "a reputable person" that bin Laden has been killed. This is usually treated as the first widely seen report. |
| Shortly after | A CBS producer and then the networks confirm it from congressional and official sources. |
| 11:35 pm | Obama announces bin Laden's death. |

So the "24 hours" is off by a factor of about 20. And by 10:24 pm the
news was spreading through Washington's phone trees, which is how Urbahn
heard it too.

### What Johnson himself says

In a November 2025 interview on *The Hollywood Reporter*'s *Awards Chatter*
podcast, Johnson said "a friend of a friend" called him with the news and
said the President would speak in about 20 minutes. He waited about 25
minutes, tweeted, and then got a second call saying Obama hadn't gone on
yet. His reaction: "Oh s**t." He didn't name the caller. One outlet notes
that he has a cousin who was a Navy SEAL, but Johnson didn't say that was
the source.

That's an account of being slightly ahead of the news through a personal
contact, then embarrassed about it. It's not an account of insider
access to an operation.

### Matan's "John Cena" interjection

Matan is mixing up a different, true story: that night, John Cena told the
crowd at WWE's *Extreme Rules* event about bin Laden's death. He did that
after the news broke, not before.

### "Controlled by deep-state factions"

- **No evidence was given** apart from the bin Laden tweet, and that doesn't show what's claimed.
- **The claim is built so that it can't lose.** "Factions within the deep state" names no agency or person. It says nothing about what being "controlled" made him do. Both his 2020 endorsement and his 2024 refusal to endorse could be read as "controlled." When any behaviour fits, the theory isn't explaining anything. See [Theories that can't lose](../thinking/theories-that-cant-lose.md).
- **His caution about politics has an ordinary explanation.** In April 2024 he told Fox News he wouldn't endorse in 2024 because his 2020 endorsement had divided his audience. He has floated a presidential run since 2017. A celebrity with a huge, politically mixed fan base, and possible ambitions, has every reason to stay neutral.
- **The frame came from the host.** Matan asked "do you think The Rock is controlled?" and Jiang agreed. Then, after saying "there's no difference between United States and Israel," Jiang said The Rock was also "Israeli-controlled." The claim was assembled on the spot, in a comedy setting, rather than argued.

### So which verdict?

**Misleading.** The kernel is real: The Rock beat the President to the
news. But the claim stretches 70 minutes into 24 hours, turns grapevine
news into insider foreknowledge, and uses that to imply control, for which
there's no evidence. "Unproven" was the other option, since "controlled"
can't be tested. But the specific evidence offered is wrong, and it leaves
the listener believing something false.

## Thinking lesson

**Check the timeline.** "24 hours before" sounds like foreknowledge of a
secret operation. Putting real times next to each event turns it into
"70 minutes before the speech, after the raid, one minute before a Rumsfeld
aide tweeted the same thing." The story doesn't need The Rock's friends in
the deep state any more.
