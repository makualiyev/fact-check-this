# Arut Nazaryan ("Arut")

## Who they are

- **Name:** Arutyun (Arut) Nazaryan, known online simply as **Arut**. Born 8 January 1988 in Namangan, Uzbekistan, according to the celebrity-profile site 24SMI. In the interview checked here he says his mother is Armenian and that he was raised a Christian.
- **What they do:** built a following as a "crypto entrepreneur" and blogger, and records rap music, including paid features with well-known Russian-language rappers. Lives in Dubai.
- **Music label:** Bugatti Music, founded in Dubai in 2022. Arut announced on 26 November 2023 that the label was closing.
- **Legal trouble:** Russian-language media reported in June 2026 that a Dubai court had sentenced him to two years in prison and a fine of 200 million dirhams for running a crypto exchange without a licence. The reports trace back to a single blogger (Andrey Alistarov), and we found no official UAE statement.

## What to keep in mind

- **No relevant expertise.** Nothing in their background suggests training in religious history, Semitic languages or theology. Their religious remarks in the interview are personal views, offered sincerely, not research.
- **Credibility of self-presentation.** In a 2024 interview with Ksenia Sobchak, as reported by Rambler, they admitted to finishing only nine school grades and buying three diplomas, and couldn't confirm being a "crypto billionaire." Worth knowing when they speak confidently about facts.
