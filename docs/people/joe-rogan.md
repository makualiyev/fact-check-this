# Joe Rogan

## Who he is

- American stand-up comedian, actor and TV host, born in 1967 in Newark,
  New Jersey. He became widely known as host of *Fear Factor* in the early
  2000s, after playing Joe Garrelli on the sitcom *NewsRadio*.
- Colour commentator for the UFC (mixed martial arts) since the late
  1990s/early 2000s.
- Since 2009 he has hosted **The Joe Rogan Experience** (JRE), long-form
  conversations that often run three hours. It has been among the most
  listened-to podcasts in the world for years, and moved to Spotify under an
  exclusive deal in 2020.

## What to keep in mind

- **He is the host, not the expert.** Guests range from scientists and
  historians to comedians and conspiracy broadcasters, and he often adds his
  own takes on their subjects.
- **Psychedelics are a recurring theme.** He talks openly about his own DMT
  and psilocybin experiences and often cites the psychiatrist Rick
  Strassman and the writer Terence McKenna. Claims about DMT and the brain
  come up on the show again and again.
- **He sometimes checks things live.** His producer, Jamie Vernon, pulls up
  photos and articles during episodes. That helps, but a quick image search
  isn't the same as a check.
- **Criticism.** In January 2022, 270 doctors, scientists and health
  workers signed an open letter asking Spotify to act on COVID-19
  misinformation on the show. Supporters value its open, unscripted format.
