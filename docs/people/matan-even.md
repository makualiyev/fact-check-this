# Matan Even

## Who he is

- American comedian and YouTuber (full name reported as Matan Evenoff), known for pranks and absurdist humour.
- He first went viral as a child, in 2019, for speaking out against the Chinese government's handling of the Hong Kong protests.
- He became widely known after the 2022 Game Awards, when he walked on stage with the *Elden Ring* team, took the microphone and thanked "Bill Clinton."
- **The show:** a confrontational comedy interview podcast. Typical episodes open with deliberately nonsensical or offensive questions and mix in staged bits and "conspiracy baiting." Guests have ranged from politicians and their relatives to internet personalities.

## What to keep in mind

- **His questions are a comedy format.** Leading questions ("Do you think The Rock is controlled?") are part of the act. That matters when judging how seriously a guest meant an answer: a guest may be playing along.
- Some of his stunts have been criticised as humiliating to guests, e.g. interviewing the adult performer Bonnie Blue next to a man in a hazmat suit.
