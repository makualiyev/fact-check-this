# Sergey Minaev

## Who he is

- Russian writer, TV and radio host, screenwriter and editor, born 25 January 1975 in Moscow.
- **Education:** graduated from the Russian State University for the Humanities (RGGU) in 1998 in archival studies, according to Wikipedia. We found no advanced degree in history.
- **Novels:** his debut, *Dukhless: The Tale of an Unreal Man* (2006), was a bestseller about Moscow's office class in the 2000s. It was filmed in 2012.
- **Media work:**
    - Hosted *Honest Monday* (*Честный понедельник*) on the NTV channel, 2009–2012.
    - Made the internet talk show *Minaev Live*, 2011–2013.
    - Editor-in-chief of the Russian edition of *Esquire* from October 2016 to February 2022. The magazine has since been renamed *Rules of Life* (*Правила жизни*), and he still edits it.
- **The channel:** since around 2019 he has run the YouTube channel **МИНАЕВ LIVE** (MINAEV LIVE). Its series *Уроки истории* ("History lessons") tells historical and current-affairs stories in a polished, documentary style. Episodes are also distributed on the Russian streaming service ivi.

## What to keep in mind

- **A storyteller, not a researcher.** The episodes are well produced and often draw on real documents, but they're entertainment journalism. They don't usually cite sources on screen, and colourful asides are part of the style.
- **Advertising.** The episodes carry sponsored segments (the one checked here has a real-estate ad), so they're a commercial product as well as an explainer.
- **Framing.** The episode checked here promises "only facts, no conspiracy theories" while discussing a "world government". Treat that promise as packaging, and check the facts one at a time.
