# Jiang Xueqin ("Professor Jiang")

## Who he is

- Chinese-born Canadian educator, born 1976 in Taishan, Guangdong.
- **Education:** BA in English literature from Yale (1999). We found no degree in history or religious studies.
- **"Professor" is a self-chosen title.** He teaches high-school students. Since 2022 that has been world history, philosophy and geopolitics at Moonshot Academy, a private school in Beijing. Earlier he was a deputy principal at Shenzhen Middle School (around 2008–2010) and ran the international program at Peking University High School (around 2010–2012). Exact titles and dates vary between sources.
- **Earlier work:** In 2002, while filming a documentary on labour unrest for PBS, he was detained in China and deported, a case covered by press-freedom groups. He wrote about Chinese school reform, including the Chinese-language book *Creative China* (2014).
- **The channel:** *Predictive History* started around 2023. It describes its method as historical patterns plus game theory, inspired by Asimov's fictional "psychohistory." Its series include *Civilization*, *Geo-Strategy*, *Game Theory* and *Secret History*.

## Why people listen

The channel went viral over **"Geo-Strategy #8: The Iran Trap"** (May 2024),
which predicted that Trump would win in 2024, that the US would get drawn
into a war with Iran, and that it would lose that war. Interest spiked
after the June 2025 US strikes on Iran and again after the US–Israeli war
on Iran began in February 2026, when media called him "China's
Nostradamus." Those predictions are tracked in [Predictions](../predictions/index.md).

## What to keep in mind

- **He is an educator with a strong storytelling style, not a credentialed historian.** That doesn't make him wrong. But his claims about religion and ancient history don't come with expert authority, and should be checked against people who have it. See [Check the expertise](../thinking/check-the-expertise.md).
- **Critics push back hard on some of his work.**
    - The Free Press profiled him as a viral Iran expert who also believes secret societies (Freemasons, Jesuits, Sabbateans) steer world events.
    - Critics describe the *Secret History* finale, "Pax Judaica", as relying on antisemitic conspiracy tropes.
    - Other writers argue his correct calls were high-probability bets that prediction markets already favoured, and that he rarely attaches dates or probabilities.
- **Fan-made trackers of his predictions exist,** but they aren't affiliated with him and their numbers are inconsistent. This site scores only predictions with a clear outcome.
