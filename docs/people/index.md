# People

Speakers whose claims are checked here. Each profile covers background and
expertise, plus a scorecard generated from the verdicts. A scorecard shows
only the claims that ended up here, and claims that surprised someone are
more likely to be checked, so it is not a random sample.

<!-- auto:list -->
- [Alex Jones](alex-jones.md) — American conspiracy broadcaster, founder of InfoWars. Mixes real, documented history with claims that courts have ruled false and defamatory.
- [Arut Nazaryan ("Arut")](arut-nazaryan.md) — Armenian-Uzbek crypto blogger and rapper based in Dubai, known online as Arut. A self-made media personality, not a scholar of religion or languages.
- [Diana Walsh Pasulka](diana-walsh-pasulka.md) — Professor of religious studies at the University of North Carolina Wilmington who studies belief in UFOs and "non-human intelligence" as a modern religious phenomenon. A real scholar of religion, writing here partly as a participant.
- [Jiang Xueqin ("Professor Jiang")](jiang-xueqin.md) — Canadian educator in Beijing behind the Predictive History YouTube channel. Not a university professor; his degree is in English literature.
- [Joe Rogan](joe-rogan.md) — American comedian, UFC commentator and host of The Joe Rogan Experience, one of the world's most popular podcasts. An interviewer with wide curiosity, not an expert in the subjects he discusses.
- [Matan Even](matan-even.md) — American comedian and YouTuber whose interview show is built on absurd, provocative questions. A host, not an expert on the topics discussed.
- [Sergey Minaev](sergey-minaev.md) — Russian novelist (Dukhless), TV host and magazine editor who runs the MINAEV LIVE history channel on YouTube. A popular storyteller, not a trained historian.
