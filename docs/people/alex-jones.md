# Alex Jones

## Who he is

- American radio and online broadcaster who founded **InfoWars** in 1999.
- He became one of the best-known promoters of conspiracy theories in the US.
- He has appeared on *The Joe Rogan Experience* three times:
    - [#911](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2Gwm8dM3Z1CxcddNxNR2j8) (2017, with Eddie Bravo)
    - [#1255 "Alex Jones Returns!"](../sources/jre-1255-alex-jones-returns.md) (February 2019)
    - #1555 (October 2020, with Tim Dillon), where Rogan's producer fact-checked him live

## Track record

His record is an unusually clear example of
[credibility transfer](../thinking/credibility-transfer.md):

- **Some things he talks about are real and well documented.** Examples include Operation Paperclip (see the [scorecard](#scorecard)) and real Cold War government programmes.
- **Others are false, at great cost to real people.** For years he claimed the 2012 Sandy Hook school shooting, in which 20 children and 6 adults were killed, was staged. Families of the victims sued him for defamation.
    - In 2022, juries in Texas and Connecticut found him liable. The awards came to about $49 million in Texas and about $1.4 billion in Connecticut (with punitive damages).
    - During the 2022 Texas trial he testified that he now considered the shooting "100% real."
    - Earlier, on [JRE #1255](../sources/jre-1255-alex-jones-returns.md) in February 2019, he told Rogan he believed it happened and blamed a "mild psychosis" of thinking everything was staged.
    - He filed for bankruptcy that year.
    - In 2018 he was banned from most major platforms.

The real stories make the false ones easier to believe, so each claim he
makes has to be checked on its own.
